### Stage 4 property run at 1,000

| ID    | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Severity | Status | Reasoning or fix                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

| S4-P1 | P-rate on unseen hardware found a room breach the guard allowed: a broadcast scene lifted one driver at once while another faded out, and the room's rise and fall from one moment were counted as one leg | High     | Fixed  | Present since Stage 3 (it fails on `main` with the same seed). Uniting the faded and instant predictions merged events within 5 ms even inside one prediction, where they are opposite legs. `uniteTimes` keeps each prediction's own events; ADR 0011 already says opposite legs are never merged. The counterexample is now a fixed example in the property, and fails with the old merge. |

The DALI review of the fix passed. It noted that `mergeDirected` still counts opposite legs on one address that start within 5 ms as one leg, where ADR 0011 said opposite directions are never merged. That is kept on purpose and is now stated in ADR 0011: it hides a 1 ms sampling artefact of a constant-level colour swap, and it cannot carry a flash past the limits, because an instant swap is a hard step (one in 2 s) and a faded one takes at least 0.7 s.
